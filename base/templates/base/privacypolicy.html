Privacy Policy

Welcome to Pluence! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you access or use the Pluence platform, which connects influencers and brands for collaboration.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and other account details provided during registration.
  • Profile Data: Social media account links, content preferences, and other information relevant to platform usage.
  • Payment Information: Billing details, transaction data, and payment method details for processing payments securely.
  • Usage Data: Information about your interactions with the platform, such as pages visited, proposals shared, and communications.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our platform and services.
  • Facilitate collaborations between influencers and brands.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share it with:

  • Business Partners: To facilitate brand-influencer collaborations.
  • Service Providers: Third-party payment processors and analytics providers.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no online platform is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute data security.

5. Your Rights

You have the following rights concerning your personal data:

  • Access your personal data stored on the platform.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable.

Pluence uses cookies to enhance your experience on the platform. Cookies help us understand user behavior, improve our services, and personalize your interactions. By using the platform, you consent to our use of cookies.
